## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-luxury editorial aesthetic tailored for high-horology collectors and discerning connoisseurs. Drawing direct inspiration from the master maisons of Geneva and the Vallée de Joux, the visual atmosphere balances Swiss structural discipline with the poetic warmth of classical watchmaking catalogs.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface evokes reverence, precision, and permanence. Every touchpoint feels measured, silent, and deliberate—eschewing aggressive commercial tropes in favor of museum-grade curation. The experience mirrors stepping into an exclusive private salon: expansive margins, unhurried negative space, surgical hairline accents, and bespoke typography that dignifies the mechanical artistry of grand complications.

### Design Movement
The design movement is **Editorial Minimalism infused with Haute Horlogerie Craftsmanship**:
- **Bespoke Framing:** Delicate hairline borders and refined proportions emulate engraved presentation boxes and exhibition casebacks.
- **Architectural Breathing Room:** Generous whitespace establishes exclusivity; nothing competes for immediate visual dominance.
- **Understated Kinetic Feedback:** Restrained micro-interactions mimic the tactile resistance of winding a mechanical crown—weighty, calibrated, and seamless.

## Colors

The palette draws upon physical materials of artisanal watchmaking: brushed yellow gold, warm alabaster parchment, aged watch dials, and deep obsidian inks.

### Primary Accents & Hierarchy
- **Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`):** The primary metallic accent. Reserved for micro-details, selected states, curated badge markers, focused borders, and high-value highlights. Never used as heavy solid fills for large buttons; instead applied with surgical restraint.
- **Charcoal Black (`#202020`):** The authoritative grounding neutral. Governs primary editorial titles, primary calls-to-action, key technical metrics, and structural contrast moments.
- **Warm Beige (`#EAE2D5`):** The structural line work and subtle container tone. Used for 1px hairline dividers, technical specification grids, and card bounding boxes.
- **Warm Alabaster (`#F8F5EF`):** The global canvas background. Provides an organic, warm archival depth superior to sterile digital white.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated surface cards, floating complication viewers, and product staging pedestals.
- **Soft Grey (`#777777`):** Dedicated to technical metadata, reference serials, secondary descriptions, and non-active state markers.

## Typography

The typography system pairs high-contrast transitional serifs with architectural sans-serif precision, echoing the duality of an antique clock face encased in modern sapphire crystal.

### Font Pairing Logic
- **Playfair Display:** Governs grand editorial headlines, collection titles, and quote elements. Its delicate serifs and dramatic vertical stress celebrate tradition and narrative depth.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Drives technical specifications, interface controls, navigation, and Rupee currency formatting (`₹`). It provides crystalline legibility at diminutive sizes without visual distortion.

### Micro-Typography & Financial Representation
- **Price Displays:** Always presented with the Indian Rupee symbol formatted via `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium/semi-bold with tabular figures (`tnum`) to ensure aligned vertical scanning (e.g., `₹ 24,50,000`).
- **Uppercase Kerning:** All category kickers, reference labels, and action badges use `label-md` or `label-sm` with explicit uppercase casing and expanded tracking (+0.12em to +0.16em).

## Layout & Spacing

This layout adheres to a disciplined 12-column architectural grid on desktop, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column system on mobile devices.

### Layout Model
- **Macro Framing:** Canvas margins are purposefully expansive (`margin: 4rem` on desktop) to enclose timepiece showcases like curated art in an exhibition hall. Maximum content width is capped at 1440px to prevent visual dilution on wide displays.
- **Rhythmic Vertical Stacking:** Sections are divided by deliberate intervals (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`), forcing the eye to absorb individual movements, calibres, and provenance narratives independently.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Editorial showcases employ staggered column offsets (e.g., 7 columns for imagery paired with 5 columns for movement specifications) to maintain editorial rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows and blurred skeuomorphic fills. Instead, depth is conveyed through tactile surface layering, crisp boundaries, and diffused ambient lighting.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation):** The global canvas in Warm Alabaster (`#F8F5EF`). Completely flat and matte.
- **Tier 1 (Object Pedestal):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface cards framed by hairline borders in Warm Beige (`1px solid #EAE2D5`).
- **Tier 2 (Floating Inspections / Menus):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) flyouts, search drawers, and cart overlays with ambient, multi-layered atmospheric elevation:
  - `box-shadow: 0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(32, 32, 32, 0.04), 0px 16px 40px -8px rgba(32, 32, 32, 0.06);`
  - Encased with a delicate border: `1px solid rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.25)`.

### Border Discipline
Hairline borders (`1px`) are fundamental to the horological identity. They evoke the precision tolerances of Swiss milled steel and precious metals. Active or focused states shift from Warm Beige (`#EAE2D5`) to Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`) without increasing stroke weight.

## Shapes

The geometric framework utilizes micro-radii to evoke milled precious metals, sapphire crystal bevels, and bespoke presentation joinery.

### Corner Radius System
- **Level 1 (Soft):** Baseline radius is 0.25rem (4px). Standard elements (interactive cards, input fields, badges) employ subtle softenings that remain distinctly crisp.
- **Buttons & Tactical Elements:** Primary buttons and image containers carry a strict 2px to 4px radius.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Micro-badges indicating limited edition serials or provenance status may use full pill radiuses only when enclosing uppercase metadata at diminutive scale (`label-sm`).
- **Watch Dials & Complication Visualizers:** Strictly circular (50% border radius) to honor classical horological geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Acquisition / Reserve):** Solid Charcoal Black (`#202020`) container with Pure White text (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #202020`. Hover triggers a smooth transition to Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`) background and matching border. Height: 48px, horizontal padding: `space-xl`, tracked uppercase text.
- **Secondary Action (Inquire / View Calibre):** Transparent background, `1px solid #202020` border, Charcoal Black text. Hover transitions to Warm Beige (`#EAE2D5`) background with preserved hairline border.
- **Tertiary / Link Action:** Unboxed, Charcoal Black or Champagne Gold with a delicate bottom border positioned 4px beneath the baseline, expanding outwards on hover.

### Product Showcase Cards
- **Base Structure:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface encased in a `1px solid #EAE2D5` border.
- **Visual Staging:** High-resolution dial and movement photography situated on a neutral tinted stage.
- **Details Section:**
  - Watch Maison & Collection Name in `label-sm` (uppercase, `color: #777777`).
  - Model Name in `headline-sm` serifs (`color: #202020`).
  - Technical Subtitle (e.g., "Automatic • 41mm • Rose Gold") in `body-sm`.
  - Price Tag prominently formatted in tabular `title-md` (e.g., `₹ 38,75,000`).
- **Interaction:** Hover gently transitions the border to `rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.6)` and subtly reveals an instant "Reserve" or "Quick View" action without jarring card translations.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Architecture:** Minimalist flat fields with Warm Alabaster (`#F8F5EF`) or Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) fill, bounded by `1px solid #EAE2D5`. Height: 48px, horizontal padding: `space-md`.
- **States:** Focus produces an immediate crisp outline in Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`) without thick external glow halos.
- **Labels:** Positioned strictly above fields in `label-md` uppercase style. Placeholder text in Soft Grey (`#777777`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Checkboxes:** 18px square with 2px radius. Default border `1px solid #EAE2D5`. Checked state fills Charcoal Black (`#202020`) with a pure white checkmark icon.
- **Radio Rings:** 18px concentric circle. Checked state features a Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`) center pip enclosed within a hairline boundary.

### Technical Specification Grids
- Designed specifically for horological data (Case Diameter, Calibre, Power Reserve, Water Resistance, Complications).
- Formatted as alternate alternating table or structured grid rows split by `1px solid #EAE2D5` dividers.
- Metric labels set in muted `label-md` (`#777777`), while technical outputs sit in crisp `body-md` Charcoal Black (`#202020`).

### Chips & Filter Badges
- Unselected: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with `1px solid #EAE2D5` border, Charcoal Black text, padded `space-xs` vertically and `space-md` horizontally.
- Selected: Charcoal Black (`#202020`) background with White text and Champagne Gold micro-indicator dot.
